What is EcoFem?

Menstrual care shaped by circular thinking.

EcoFem is an innovation developing biodegradable sanitary pad prototypes that use processed water hyacinth fibres as part of the absorbent core.

The project responds to two urgent realities: the need for more affordable menstrual hygiene options and the environmental burden created by invasive water hyacinth and persistent sanitary waste.

EcoFem is currently under development. Its materials, product design and performance continue to be tested and improved in preparation for wider validation, certification and future commercialization.

Development journey

From question to tested product concept.

The journey is iterative: evidence from each stage informs the next.

01

Problem discovery

Connecting menstrual access needs with a local environmental challenge.

02

Material research

Exploring how prepared water hyacinth fibre may function in an absorbent structure.

03

Prototype development

Building early pad concepts and refining the construction method.

04

Testing and learning

Comparing performance and identifying areas requiring improvement.

05

Validation and readiness

Preparing for stronger product evidence, pilot learning and regulatory processes.
