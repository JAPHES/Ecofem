Sustainable menstrual care, naturally.

The challenge

Three connected problems. One thoughtful response.

Access, waste and invasive biomass call for solutions that work together—not in isolation.

01

Menstrual access

Cost and availability can make reliable menstrual hygiene products difficult to access for many women and girls.

02

Persistent waste

Conventional sanitary products often contain non-biodegradable materials that can remain in the environment long after use.

03

Invasive biomass

Water hyacinth can disrupt waterways and livelihoods, yet its fibrous biomass may also hold useful material potential.

From invasive biomass
to useful material
Our solution

Reimagining a local resource for menstrual care.

EcoFem processes water hyacinth fibres for use as part of the absorbent core in a biodegradable sanitary pad prototype.

The product is being designed toward a careful balance of performance, comfort, affordability and reduced environmental impact.

Affordable by design Comfortable Absorbent Environmentally conscious Locally developed Resource-efficient

How EcoFem works

A material journey with purpose

Each stage is being refined through research, prototyping and testing.

01

Responsible harvesting

Water hyacinth biomass is collected with environmental responsibility in mind.

02

Fibre extraction

Useful fibres are separated from the prepared plant material.

03

Fibre treatment

Fibres are cleaned and processed for material development.

04

Core development

Treated fibre is developed as part of an absorbent core structure.

05

Prototype & testing

Pad prototypes are assembled, compared, evaluated and improved.
